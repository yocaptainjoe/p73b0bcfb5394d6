Nicki, thank you for taking the time to listen. It means a lot. I picked these tracks with you in mind, and I’d love to hear your take on one. Go with whichever one you’re feeling.

I’ve been trying different sounds on this project. A lot of the songs have an upbeat rhythm and vibe, but the lyrics are darker. The word that ties it together is contrast. The album starts in Casa de Campo during the day and ends up in Boca Chica at night.

We’re going for a distinct creative direction for the visuals. Our animation team is building a world for the music videos and visualizers, with characters and settings that carry across the album.
